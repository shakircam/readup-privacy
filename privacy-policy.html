Privacy Policy for ReadUp

Last updated: October 28, 2026

ReadUp is an English learning app for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information ReadUp collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We collect only what the app needs to work, and we never sell your data.

1. Using ReadUp without an account

You can read articles, look up words, scan pages and practise speaking without signing in. Your reading history, saved words, bookmarks, settings and speaking recordings are then stored only on your device.

2. Information we collect

When you sign in with Google

If you sign in, we receive your name and email address from your Google account and create a ReadUp account for you. We use them to identify your account and to greet you in the app.

Data synced to your account

While you are signed in, the following is stored on our servers so it is backed up and available on your other devices:

  • Your profile: name, email, English level and chosen learning focus.
  • Bookmarks, including the article's title, text and original link.
  • Saved vocabulary words and the article each word came from.
  • Which speaking practices and challenge days you have completed.

PDFs you open

Signed-in accounts can open a limited number of different PDFs. To count them, we store a fingerprint of each PDF you open: a one-way code made from the file. The fingerprint cannot be turned back into the document, and the PDF itself never leaves your device.

AI summaries

When you ask for an AI summary, the text of that article is sent to our server, which asks Google's Gemini AI to summarise it. The summary is saved so the same article can be summarised again quickly. We count how many summaries each account uses, to apply fair-use limits.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a notification token for your device, linked to a random ID created by the app, so we can send you learning reminders and important updates. Daily reading reminders are scheduled on your device itself.

App usage and crash information

We use Firebase Analytics to understand which features are used (for example, which screens are opened) and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash and error reports, including your device model and operating system version. This helps us fix problems and improve the app. We do not use this information for advertising.

3. What stays on your device

  • Speaking practice recordings are saved only on your device.
  • Scanned pages are read on your device. Text recognition runs on your phone, and the images are not uploaded.
  • PDF files are read on your device and are never uploaded.
  • The Bangla dictionary is downloaded to your device and searched there.

4. Content from other websites

ReadUp shows news articles, podcasts and videos from their publishers. To show them, your device connects directly to those websites, so they may receive your IP address like any website you visit. Videos are played with YouTube's player, which is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

6. What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, synced data, PDF fingerprints, AI summary history and notification tokens are deleted. Crash and usage reports are kept by Firebase for a limited period under its own retention settings.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data at any time in the app: More → Delete account. You can also request deletion here: Account deletion. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

9. Children's privacy

ReadUp is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and each account can access only its own data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how ReadUp handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
