Delete your ReadUp account

You can permanently delete your ReadUp account and its data at any time. Here's what happens and how to do it.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open ReadUp and go to the More tab.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm in the dialog that appears.

Your account and synced data are removed immediately — there is no waiting period.

What gets deleted

  • Your ReadUp sign-in account (email/password or Google).
  • Your profile, including your reading level and focus-pack selection.
  • Your saved bookmarks stored on our servers.
  • Your saved vocabulary stored on our servers.

What is kept

Reading data saved locally on your device stays on your device. Deleting your account only removes what is stored on our servers. To remove the on-device data as well, uninstall the app.

Prefer to request it directly?

If you can't access the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you: shakircam@gmail.com. Please use the subject line ReadUp account deletion request.
